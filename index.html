How does this work?

The pool in one breath

Every week you pick the winner of all 16 games and rank your picks 1–16 by how sure you are (16 = most confident). Get a pick right, you earn that many points. Get it wrong, you earn 0. Most points wins the week. Top 8 get paid. Ties are settled by whoever guessed closest to the total points scored in the Monday night game.

The Crew

The eight of us, pulled out of the crowd so you don't have to scroll. Each tile shows:

  • Big number / small number — points you've earned so far / the most you could still finish with.
  • Rank — where you sit in the whole pool right now. Green means you're in the top 8. A "T" means you're tied.
  • Left — how many of your picks haven't finished yet.
  • Proj. — where the computer expects you to finish once the week plays out.
  • Top 8 — your odds of finishing in the money (see below).

Below the tiles is the picks grid: every game, everyone's pick and the points on it. Green = pick won. Red = pick lost. Dashed green = game in progress and your team is ahead. Dashed red = in progress and behind. Grey = hasn't kicked off. The bottom rows total it up and show each person's Monday night tiebreaker guess.

Games

One card per matchup with the score and status. The bar shows how the pool split: the longer side is where more people went. Under each team is the share of the pool on it and the average points they risked. A game where 90% of people are on one side at 12 points is a "chalk" game; a 55/45 split is where the week gets decided.

Standings

Everybody in the pool, live. Tap a column header to sort, or type a name to find someone.

  • Pts — points from finished games, plus games where your pick is currently leading. It can go down if a team blows a lead.
  • Max — your total if every remaining pick hits. If your Max is below 8th place's Pts, your week is over.
  • Left — unfinished picks.
  • MNF — the tiebreaker guess. Once Monday night ends you'll see ± how far off it was.
  • Top 8 — odds of finishing in the money.

The gold line is the pay line: above it you get paid, below it you don't. Crew members are highlighted in green.

What "Top 8 %" actually means

The page plays out the rest of the week 5,000 times. For each unfinished game it uses ESPN's win probability (the live in-game number once a game starts; the betting line before kickoff) to decide who wins, then re-scores everyone and checks who lands in the top 8. Your percentage is how often you made it. So 40% means "in 2,000 of 5,000 pretend weekends, you got paid." Before Sunday it's mostly about how much chalk you took; by Sunday night it's mostly math on the games left. When all games are final it's simply 100% or 0%.

How it stays current

  • Picks: Mike's system emails the locked workbook Thursday evening. It's loaded onto this site automatically that night.
  • Scores: pulled straight from ESPN while the page is open, roughly every 45 seconds during games. Tap ↻ to force it.
  • Weeks: the dropdown up top lets you look back at any week that's been loaded.

Fine print

Scoring rules were checked against Mike's official Week 1 results and match exactly. One assumption: a tied NFL game pays nobody. This site isn't the official scoreboard, Mike's is. If the two ever disagree, Mike wins.
